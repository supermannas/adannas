How I lead creative and learning

Operating model
  • Find the human question inside the brief, then build a clear narrative around it.
  • Make complex tools and ideas usable for people with different levels of experience.
  • Give teams a shared workflow, clear ownership, and room to make strong work.
  • Test, listen, revise, and finish. The system should serve the people doing the work.
What you can expect
  • Creative direction grounded in practical execution.
  • Storytelling across film, video, writing, presentations, web, and learning experiences.
  • Calm, direct leadership that makes change easier to enter and easier to sustain.

Systems that protect the work

Standards
  • Clear narrative, voice, and quality standards for every audience.
  • Accessibility through captions, plain language, readable structure, and thoughtful design.
  • Editorial and technical review that protects accuracy without flattening the story.
Workflow
  • Briefs, milestones, feedback loops, and clear decisions.
  • Reusable templates and workflows that let teams spend more time on judgment and craft.
  • Small experiments, honest assessment, and steady improvement.
